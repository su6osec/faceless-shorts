# Viral Hooks & High-Retention Scriptwriting Blueprint

The algorithm of YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok measures one key metric above all others: **Average Percentage Viewed (APV)**. If your APV is above 75%, the video is pushed into the viral feed; if it reaches 100%+, it goes multi-million viral.

To hit 80%+ APV consistently, every faceless short must strictly follow this 4-phase anatomy.

---

## The 4-Phase Short-Form Anatomy

```
[0s - 3s]       PHASE 1: THE PATTERN INTERRUPT HOOK (Must stop the thumb)
[3s - 12s]      PHASE 2: THE SETUP & PROMISE (Establish the stakes)
[12s - 45s]     PHASE 3: HIGH-DENSITY RAPID PAYOFF (3-5 punchy beats, zero fluff)
[45s - 55s]     PHASE 4: THE VIRAL LOOP OR CALL-TO-ACTION (Keep APV > 100%)
```

---

## 1. The 3-Second Hook Formulas (Proven Viral Openers)

Never open with introductions ("Hi guys", "Welcome back", "Today we are talking about"). Start in media res (in the middle of the action).

### Formula A: The Contrarian Truth / Disbelief Hook
* "Everything you were taught about [Topic] is a calculated lie."
* "The reason you are always exhausted has nothing to do with sleep."
* "Smart people never argue with fools, and Marcus Aurelius explained exactly why."

### Formula B: The Pattern Interrupt / Warning Hook
* "If someone stares at your forehead while you speak, look away immediately."
* "Stop scrolling right now if you want to protect your dopamine."
* "In 1962, CIA scientists tested this on 50 prisoners—and banned it immediately."

### Formula C: The Psychological Diagnosis Hook
* "If you have ever felt someone watching you in an empty room, your brain was doing this."
* "Your subconscious mind makes decisions seven seconds before you even realize it."
* "Here is the exact psychological trick narcissists use to make you apologize for their mistakes."

### Formula D: The Cosmic / Existential Dread Hook
* "There are billions of frozen rogue planets hurtling through pitch-black space right now."
* "If the Sun disappeared this second, you would not know for eight minutes and twenty seconds."

## 2. Word Count and Pacing Rules

| Duration | Target Word Count | Speaking Rate | Sentence Length |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **30 Seconds** | 65 - 75 words | Normal (+0% to +5%) | 6 - 10 words per beat |
| **45 Seconds** | 95 - 110 words | Snappy (+5% to +10%) | 7 - 12 words per beat |
| **55 Seconds** | 120 - 135 words | Fast (+10%) | 8 - 14 words per beat |

* **Cut Every Filler Word**: Eliminate "actually", "basically", "literally", "as you know", "in order to".
* **One Idea Per Sentence**: Never use complex compound sentences with multiple clauses.

## 3. The Seamless Infinite Loop (APV Booster)

The most effective technique to push YouTube Shorts and TikTok APV beyond 100% is the **Infinite Loop**, where the final sentence seamlessly flows into the very first sentence without a break.

### Loop Example 1 (Dark Psychology):
* **Hook (Sentence 1)**: *Why do toxic people always come back into your life?*
* **Body**: *(Explains intermittent reinforcement and ego validation)*
* **Ending (Sentence Final)**: *They do not miss you. They only miss the control they had over you, and that is exactly...*
* **(Loops back to Sentence 1)**: *...Why do toxic people always come back into your life?*

### Loop Example 2 (Stoicism):
* **Hook (Sentence 1)**: *Marcus Aurelius had one brutal rule for inner peace.*
* **Body**: *(Explains letting go of what you cannot control)*
* **Ending (Sentence Final)**: *If you want to be completely unshakable, remember...*
* **(Loops back to Sentence 1)**: *...Marcus Aurelius had one brutal rule for inner peace.*

* **Visual Style**: Dark moody cinema noir, high-contrast chiaroscuro, silhouettes, glowing eyes, hourglass, smoke.
* **Voice**: Deep, steady, mysterious (`en-US-ChristopherNeural`).

* **Voice**: Serious, investigative (`en-GB-SoniaNeural` or `en-US-ChristopherNeural`).
