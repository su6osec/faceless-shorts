# Visual Prompt Engineering Guide for 9:16 Vertical Video

When generating AI visuals for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok, the visual must instantly grab the viewer's attention and complement the spoken sentence.

---

## 1. The Anatomy of a High-CTR Visual Prompt

Every visual prompt should follow this 5-component structure:

```
[Subject & Action] + [Environment & Atmosphere] + [Lighting & Color Grading] + [Camera Perspective] + [Quality Modifiers]
```

### Example Breakdown:
* **Subject**: *A weathered marble bust of Marcus Aurelius with golden cracks (kintsugi style)*
* **Environment**: *Inside an ancient storm-swept Roman temple with lightning reflecting on marble floors*
* **Lighting**: *Dramatic chiaroscuro, volumetric rim lighting, cold blue and warm gold contrasts*
* **Perspective**: *Intense low-angle macro portrait shot, vertical 9:16 composition*
* **Quality**: *Photorealistic, 8k wallpaper, cinema anamorphic lens, octane render*

## 3. Top Keywords to Boost Flux / SDXL Visual Quality

* **Lighting**: `volumetric lighting`, `chiaroscuro`, `cinematic rim lighting`, `golden hour`, `bioluminescent glow`
* **Optics & Lens**: `35mm anamorphic lens`, `macro shallow depth of field`, `f/1.4 aperture`, `bokeh`
* **Renderer Aesthetics**: `photorealistic`, `8k wallpaper`, `octane render`, `unreal engine 5 cinematic`
* **Framing**: `vertical 9:16 composition`, `low-angle dramatic portrait`, `centered hero subject`
